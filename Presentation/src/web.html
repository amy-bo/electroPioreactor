Sustainable Protein for All

Protein made without the land, water and emissions that livestock needs.

We are working on hydrogen-oxidising bacteria (HOB): microbes that turn hydrogen, oxygen and carbon dioxide into protein, so with water and electricity they make protein from air.

Not for profit, with open source bioreactors, so anyone, anywhere could make protein locally.

Mission statement

AMYBO exists to end world hunger, poverty, and the biodiversity and climate crises by making delicious, wholesome, sustainable protein universally accessible.

Ending world hunger: protein from air needs no farmland or particular climate, so it can be made wherever it is needed, whatever the weather.

Ending poverty: affordable, open designs let protein be made, owned and sold locally, keeping the income where the food is eaten.

Ending the biodiversity crisis: protein from air needs no farmland, so we could rewild the 83% of the world's farmland that supplies only 18% of our calories.

Ending the climate crisis: protein from air and renewable electricity could save over 10% of global greenhouse gas emissions, animal protein's current share.

Delicious: a joy to eat, with no off-putting tastes or textures, or nobody will switch.

Wholesome: all essential amino acids, no toxins and no cholesterol, so better for you than animal products.

Sustainable protein: powered by renewable energy, hydrogen-oxidising bacteria make 10–700 times more protein per hectare than plants or animals, using 10–100 times less water and no pesticides, herbicides or antibiotics, and producing no methane or other pollution.

Universally accessible: we are developing open source bioreactors and a financial model to let everyone, everywhere enjoy sustainable protein.
