# Aseptic electroPioreactor AEP0.2 — Bill of Materials

The AEP0.2 bill of materials now lives in the electroPioreactor guide at <https://docs.electroPioreactor.org/AEP>, as one component file per part under [`docs/components/`](../../../docs/components/) in this repository: each file records its original BoM item number and Critical / Generic tier in its first changelog note, the specification as `parameters`, and the supplier where the BoM gave one; the steps that use each part are in [`docs/steps/`](../../../docs/steps/). The open specifications below are kept here until they are resolved.
