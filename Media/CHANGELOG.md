## 2026-06-25

- [x] **Buffered pH & bleaching model → new Chemistry tab** (uncommitted) — replaced the old "buffer holds pH" assumption (which sized CO₂ dosing to O₂ venting rather than to carbon demand) with a real open-system carbonate+phosphate **charge-balance pH solve**, on a dedicated **Chemistry** sheet (7th tab) — the equilibrium maths is graduate physical chemistry, out of place in Biology and clearer for the third-party electrochemist. Each medium's full ionic recipe is entered as published, sourced cells (g/L + handbook molar masses); Excel derives the strong-ion difference, total phosphate, ammonium and chloride by `media_sel`. pH is the root of the charge residual over a transparent trial-pH grid (no circular reference). Equilibrium constants are **van't Hoff–corrected to the reactor's 30 °C** (same treatment the sheet already uses for Henry constants). The HOCl *fraction* (meaningless alone) was replaced by an **anodic bleaching estimate**: free-chlorine evolution from the Gerrit-Law current at FE=1 (conservative ceiling, since FE can't exceed 1), gated on the medium actually containing chloride — turning the dormant "chloride-free" assumption into a live warning. A note on the pH cell flags that it's the full-sparge trough (a lagging probe may miss it). Computed for UdG: pH 6.31 at full CO₂ sparge (air gives 8.0). (The FE=1 free-chlorine ceiling introduced in this entry was subsequently found unphysical — chlorine evolution from trace chloride is mass-transport-limited to a small fraction of the current, and medium ammonium scavenges free chlorine — and the bleaching model is being rebuilt; see later entries.) Biology trimmed of the relocated model; `media_sel` dropdown repointed to Chemistry; stale `calcChain` dropped (rebuilds on load).
