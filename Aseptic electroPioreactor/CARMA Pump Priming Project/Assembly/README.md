# Aseptic electroPioreactor AEP0.1 Assembly instructions

## Required Tools

1. Computer with SD card reader
2. microSD to SD adapter
3. Philips PH0 Screwdriver
4. Gas cylinder wrench
5. Cryogenic gloves (rated to at least -80°C)
6. Eye/face protection
7. Lab coat
8. Other PPE as directed by your supervisor/department/employer

## Method

1. Connect empty dovetail platforms in raft
   1. 250ml duran media bottle at rear
   2. 250ml duran product bottle next to media bottle (alternate media and waste if multiple AEPs)
   3. Sodastream in front of media bottle
   4. Peristaltic pumps in front of Sodastream
   5. Pioreactor in front of Peristaltic pumps
2. Follow relevant Pioreactor hardware setup guide: https://docs.pioreactor.com/user-guide/20ml-v11-hardware-setup-intro
3. Follow relevant Pioreactor software setup guide: https://docs.pioreactor.com/user-guide/software-set-up
4. Set up electrolysis
   1. Insert electrodes half-way into Vial Cap
   2. Push electrodes fully into their Electrode Top Stop ***(Martin to design and print)***
   3. Temporarily place the Seating Guide ***(Martin to design and print)*** on top of the Vial Cap between the electrodes
   4. Push down the Electrode Top Stop until it is flush with the Seating Guide which is flush with the top of the Vial Cap
   5. Add nutrient solution to the Vial
   6. Fully screw the Vial Cap onto the Vial
   7. Without raising the Electrode Top Stop, slide out the Seating Guide
   8. The electrodes should now protrude into the vial to the standard depth
   9. Connect electrodes to ***(Gerrit to describe)***
   10. Check electrolysis by ***(Martin to describe)***
   11. Insert vial into Pioreactor once satisfied all vials have even electrolysis
