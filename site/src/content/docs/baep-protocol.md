A BAEP runs exactly as an MEP: follow the [MEP protocol](/MEP/protocol/). To build one, follow the [BAEP guide](/BAEP/).

With the vent filters fitted, take a gas outlet's filter off only for as long as it takes to inoculate or sample through that luer, then refit it.
