The electroPioreactor is made by AMYBO, which works on sustainable protein from hydrogen-oxidising bacteria (HOB). The Aseptic electroPioreactor (AEP) is being developed with Imperial College London and the University of Edinburgh under CARMA Hub Pump Priming funding, and is sold in the UK by LabCrafter.

## AMYBO

[AMYBO](https://amybo.org) created the electroPioreactor to make it possible to produce HOB affordably, safely and locally, as a sustainable alternative protein. <!-- source: README.md; AsepticElectroPioreactor/README.md (Origins) -->

AMYBO's strapline is "Sustainable Protein for All". It was founded in 2023 by Dr Martin Currie and is, for now, a not-for-profit trading name of his company, andeye Ltd. <!-- CHECK: strapline from brain2 AMYBO/AMYBO.md (Branding); founding year, founder and andeye Ltd trading-name status from brain2 AMYBO/amybo_org-story-draft.md (state authored, provisional). Not stated in any repo README; confirm before publishing. Martin Currie's name is public in AsepticElectroPioreactor/CARMA_PumpPriming/Results/AEP0.1 Training/README.md and AEP-Plugin/LICENSE.txt -->

AMYBO publishes its designs openly: see [Open source](/open-source/). It runs a community [forum](https://forum.amybo.org), including a [Journal Club](https://forum.amybo.org/c/journal-club) on the research literature. <!-- source: Literature/README.md -->

## The CARMA Hub Pump Priming project

The AEP is funded under [CARMA Hub Pump Priming](https://carmahub.co.uk/about-us/pump-priming-projects/#:~:text=Affordable%20Aseptic%20Electro-Bioreactor%20for%20Reproducible%20Hydrogen%20Oxidising%20Bacteria%20(HOB)%20Research), in a project titled "Affordable Aseptic Electro-Bioreactor for Reproducible Hydrogen Oxidising Bacteria (HOB) Research". Its aim is a low-cost bioreactor that keeps a single strain free of contamination, so HOB research can be repeated from lab to lab. <!-- source: AsepticElectroPioreactor/README.md; README.md -->

<!-- source: AsepticElectroPioreactor/README.md (CARMA Hub Pump Priming) -->

The first AEP0.1 prototypes were assembled and trained on by Imperial and Edinburgh students at Edinburgh University's Chris French Laboratory in November 2025. The results are on [Research](/research/#carma-project-results). <!-- source: AsepticElectroPioreactor/CARMA_PumpPriming/Results/AEP0.1 Training/README.md -->

Separately, two Mixed-culture electroPioreactors (MEP) were produced for the 2026 MSc Industrial Projects of the [School of Biological Sciences](https://biology.ed.ac.uk/) at the University of Edinburgh. <!-- source: MixedElectroPioreactor/README.md -->

## Buying from LabCrafter and Pioreactor

The electroPioreactor is built on the open-source [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) bioreactor. In the UK, the Pioreactor and the AEP kit are supplied by [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk); in the rest of the world, the Pioreactor is supplied by [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com). See [Get or build one](/get-one/). <!-- source: AsepticElectroPioreactor/README.md (Why an AEP?) -->
