An open-source electro-bioreactor that uses renewable electricity to grow protein-rich bacteria from water and carbon dioxide. It’s built on the [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com), and the designs, bill of materials and software are [freely available](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor).

## Funding and partners

AEP0.2 is an aseptic upgrade to [AMYBO](https://amybo.org)’s original electroPioreactor, funded by a [CARMA Hub Pump Priming](https://carmahub.co.uk/about-us/pump-priming-projects/) project led by [Dr Sonja Billerbeck](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/s.billerbeck) (Imperial College London) and [Prof. Chris French](https://biology.ed.ac.uk/chris-french-laboratory) (University of Edinburgh), with [AMYBO](https://amybo.org) as designer and trainer and [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk) as manufacturer.
