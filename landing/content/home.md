A sealed, open-source electro-bioreactor that uses renewable electricity to grow protein-rich bacteria from water and carbon dioxide. It’s built on the [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com), and the designs, bill of materials and assembly guide are all on GitHub.

## Funding and partners

The Aseptic electroPioreactor is funded by [CARMA Hub Pump Priming](https://carmahub.co.uk/about-us/pump-priming-projects/), led by [Dr Sonja Billerbeck](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/s.billerbeck) (Imperial College London) and [Prof. Chris French](https://biology.ed.ac.uk/chris-french-laboratory) (University of Edinburgh), with [AMYBO](https://amybo.org) as designer and trainer and [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk) as the UK vendor.
