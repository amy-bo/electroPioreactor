You build an electroPioreactor yourself, from a [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) plus electrodes, a printed vial cap and a CO₂ gas train. The [Assembly Video Guide](https://docs.electropioreactor.org/AEP/) takes you through it step by step.

## Buy the parts

- **In the UK,** [LabCrafter](https://labcrafter.co.uk) supplies the Pioreactor and produced AEP0.2 kits for Imperial College London and the University of Edinburgh.
- **Elsewhere,** [Pioreactor](https://pioreactor.com) supplies the Pioreactor, and you source the electroPioreactor parts from the parts list in the guide.
- **Buy locally:** SodaStream blue screw-in CO₂ cylinders (not the pink quick-connect type), two 250 ml GL45 glass bottles, nutrient solution and your bacteria.

## Build it

Each step of the guide has a short video, a parts list and checks. [Before you start](https://docs.electropioreactor.org/AEP/step-00-before-you-start/) lists every part you should have. There is a separate [guide for the MEP](https://docs.electropioreactor.org/MEP/).

To print the parts yourself, the designs are in [Components/](repo:Components/). Plan for at least two sessions, because the thread sealant on the gas train takes 24 hours to reach full strength.

> **Safety:** Electrolysis makes hydrogen, which is explosive in air at 4 to 75% by volume, and CO₂ is an asphyxiant at high concentrations. Work in a well-ventilated room, keep ignition sources away and avoid sealing the vial's headspace.

## Get help

Ask on the [AMYBO forum](https://forum.amybo.org) or email [hello@amybo.org](mailto:hello@amybo.org). If a part is missing from a kit, contact your supplier first. If you find a mistake, open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/amy-bo/electroPioreactor/issues).
